An innovative AR system that brings luxury furniture directly into customers' homes through their mobile devices. The system uses WebXR for precise spatial mapping and realistic lighting estimation.

## Technical Challenge

Achieving photorealistic rendering on mobile devices while maintaining 60fps performance required extensive shader optimization and asset compression.

## Solution

Custom PBR materials with baked lighting, combined with aggressive LOD systems and texture compression, delivered console-quality visuals on mid-range mobile devices.
