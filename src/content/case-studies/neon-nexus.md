# Neon Nexus — Live Brand Activation

A flagship brand activation for one of LatAm's largest telecom providers, designed to celebrate the launch of their 5G network across Mexico City. The installation invited visitors to step into a 12-meter diameter circular arena where their physical presence drove a real-time generative visual experience.

## The Brief

The client wanted to demonstrate 5G's low-latency capabilities in a visceral, memorable way. Our job: prove that the network is so fast, your body becomes the controller. No wearables, no apps, no friction.

## The Solution

We deployed a custom 24-camera volumetric capture rig with sub-frame synchronization, feeding skeletal tracking data through a custom MediaPipe pipeline into our WebGL particle engine. Every gesture, every shift in weight, every breath registered as a force on a 200,000-particle volumetric cloud, sculpted by signed distance fields (SDF) for buttery-smooth performance.

The visual language leaned into the brand's signature neon-cyan palette, rendered in HDR through a calibrated projection array. An ambient generative score composed by a sound designer friend responded to the same motion data, so the experience was truly multi-sensory: you didn't just see your movement, you heard it.

## The Impact

- 3-day pop-up activation reached 18,400 unique visitors
- Average dwell time: 4 minutes 22 seconds (industry benchmark: 45 seconds)
- 96% positive sentiment in post-visit surveys
- Campaign won Gold at Cannes Lions Entertainment (2025)
- Featured in Fast Company's "10 Most Innovative Brand Experiences of 2025"
