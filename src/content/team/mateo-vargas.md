# Mateo Vargas — Creative Director

Mateo founded LabXR in 2019 after a decade leading installation art collectives across Mexico City, Berlin, and São Paulo. His work sits at the intersection of physical architecture and digital light — he believes the next decade of brand experience will be defined by installations that respond to presence, not screens that demand attention.

## Background

- BFA Visual Arts, UNAM (2009)
- MFA Design and Technology, Parsons (2013)
- Speaker, SXSW, OFFF, and Ars Electronica
- Former technical director for 3 major LatAm music festivals

## Notable Work

- Creative direction for "Echoes of the Maya" (2024) — now a permanent installation at the National Museum of Anthropology
- Lead creative for "Neon Nexus" (2025) — Cannes Lions Gold winner
- Concept design for 14 permanent brand installations across LatAm
