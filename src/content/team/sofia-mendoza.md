# Sofia Mendoza — 3D & WebGL Artist

Sofia is the visual heart of our 3D pipeline. A classically trained 3D artist who learned to ship in the browser, she balances photorealism with performance budgets — every material she creates ships with a documented "what we cut and why" rationale.

## Background

- BFA Digital Animation, Universidad de las Américas (2015)
- 7 years at major game studios before joining LabXR (2022)
- Published artist, Behance 3D category (40K+ followers)
- Specialist in glTF optimization for low-end devices

## Notable Work

- Lead 3D artist for "AeroFlow" (2025) — 4M polygon car model instanced to 240K on screen
- Material library for "Lumina Retail AR" (2024) — physically-based gold and leather that render correctly under LiDAR lighting
- Hero assets for 3 Awwwards-featured WebGL experiences
