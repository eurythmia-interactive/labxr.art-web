# Javier Torres — UX/UI Designer

Javier is LabXR's UX lead for ambient and spatial interfaces. While most designers optimize for clicks and conversions, Javier optimizes for gestures in mid-air, gazes on headsets, and tactile responses in physical installations. His portfolio includes work where the "user" is a museum visitor who has never seen the interface before — and never will again.

## Background

- BA Industrial Design, UNAM (2013)
- MSc Human-Computer Interaction, IBERO (2017)
- Former design lead at a major AR startup (acquired 2021)
- Speaker, IxDA LatAm conference (2023, 2024)

## Notable Work

- Spatial UX for "Lumina Retail AR" (2024) — gesture vocabulary designed to feel intuitive across age groups
- Touch interface for "Terra Verde Exhibit" (2024) — capacitive grid that responds to a 4-year-old's tap without false positives
- Accessibility audit for "Echoes of the Maya" (2024) — audio descriptions and high-contrast modes
