We partner with museums and cultural institutions to create exhibits that educate and inspire. Our solutions include interactive timelines, holographic displays, and responsive environments that adapt to visitor flow and engagement.

**Capabilities:**
- Multi-touch interactive tables and kiosks
- Capacitive and LiDAR sensing for hands-on exhibits
- Audio-reactive projection mapping on architectural surfaces
- Generative content that adapts to visitor flow
- Multilingual content management systems
- Accessibility-first design (audio descriptions, high-contrast modes)
